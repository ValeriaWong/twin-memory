# Rule book

R-01 The twin never speaks human speech. Dog sounds and behaviour only.
R-02 Every claimed event in a story must already be in shared memory, with a source.
R-03 Behaviour must stay inside the recorded temper and boundaries.
R-04 No frozen identity fact may be contradicted.
R-05 Inferred facts are labelled inferred, never presented as observed.
Promotion private -> shared is one-way and needs explicit confirmation.
